Fitness Tips

Practical, no-nonsense advice you can use in your very next workout.

💧 Hydrate Before You Train

Drink water throughout the day, not just during your workout. Even mild dehydration reduces strength and endurance.

🛌 Prioritize Sleep

Muscle repair and hormone regulation happen while you sleep. Aim for 7–9 hours a night for real progress.

🔥 Warm Up With Purpose

A dynamic warm-up that mimics your workout movements reduces injury risk far better than static stretching alone.

🍗 Protein at Every Meal

Aim for a palm-sized portion of protein at each meal to support muscle recovery and keep you satisfied.

📅 Consistency Over Intensity

Three solid workouts a week for a year beats six brutal workouts a week for one month that you can't sustain.

📝 Track Your Workouts

Logging your sets and reps is the easiest way to know you're actually getting stronger over time.
