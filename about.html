About Kevin

Certified personal trainer helping Boston clients build strength, confidence, and sustainable habits.

My Story

I'm Kevin Vu, a certified personal trainer based in Boston, MA. I started coaching after seeing how much a good program and honest guidance can change someone's relationship with fitness. My approach focuses on building habits that last well beyond a single training block.

I work with clients of every experience level, from people picking up their first set of dumbbells to competitive athletes chasing a new personal record.
