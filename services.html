Support Services

Everything available to you as a KV Fitness Coaching client, organized by category with hours of operation.

Client Support Categories

Support is organized into four major categories below. Each lists its available hours.

  1. In-Person Training Sessions

    One-on-one and small-group sessions held at Kevin's partner gym facility in downtown Boston.

    • Monday – Friday: 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM
    • Saturday: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM
    • Sunday: Closed

  2. Nutrition Coaching Support

    Meal plan reviews, grocery guidance, and check-in responses for Performance and Elite clients.

    • Monday – Friday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
    • Response time: within 24 hours

  3. Recovery & Mobility Services

    Guided stretching sessions and mobility assessments to reduce injury risk and speed up recovery.

    • Tuesday & Thursday: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM
    • By appointment only

  4. Virtual Coaching & Messaging

    Remote check-ins, video form reviews, and messaging support for clients who train off-site.

    • Monday – Friday: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM
    • Weekend messaging (Elite tier only): 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Additional Boston-Area Resources

A few local options clients often ask about, in addition to the services above.

  • MapMyFitness — track outdoor runs around the Charles River
  • Boston Sports Medicine — physical therapy referrals
  • Whole Foods Market, Back Bay — nutrition-friendly grocery shopping
